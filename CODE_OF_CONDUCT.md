# Code of Conduct

LicoMesh welcomes contributors and users from different backgrounds and levels
of experience. Project spaces must remain safe, technical, and constructive.

## Expected Conduct

- Treat other participants with respect and assume good faith while evidence is
  still being gathered.
- Critique code, behavior, and documented claims rather than people.
- Keep reviews specific, actionable, and proportionate to the risk involved.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, private messages,
  credentials, runtime data, or vulnerability details without authorization.
- Accept maintainer decisions on scope and moderation, while using the normal
  appeal path when a decision needs reconsideration.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, impersonation, doxxing, and deliberate disclosure of secrets or
private security reports are not permitted in project-controlled spaces.

## Reporting and Enforcement

For ordinary moderation issues, contact the maintainers through a private
maintainer channel published by the repository host. Do not open a public issue
when the report contains personal information or would expose another person.
Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will limit report access to people needed for review, preserve
evidence only as long as necessary, hear affected participants where practical,
and respond according to severity. Responses may include a private warning,
content removal, temporary participation limits, or permanent exclusion from
project-controlled spaces. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself
a violation.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, and other spaces represented as official LicoMesh community
spaces.
